# Bar Check

## v1.2.1
- The Bar Check! button now opens a small menu to pick where to post: Say, Guild, Yell or Party.
- Guild and Party post right away. Say and Yell put the line in your chat box so you press Enter (the game only lets addons speak aloud inside instances).
- `/bc shout yell` works too, and in a dungeon finder group the line goes to instance chat.
