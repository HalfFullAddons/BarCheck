BAR CHECK  (v1.2.0)

A progress card for your character: level, XP "bars in" (with rested XP), dungeons in reach for your level, your professions, and a stats strip. Open it, snip it, paste it into Discord.

INSTALL
Copy the "BarCheck" folder into your WoW Forever Interface\AddOns folder (restart the game if it was running).

USE
  /bc or /barcheck      show or hide your card in the middle of the screen
  /bc <name>            show a character you have logged in on before
  /bc list              list saved characters
  /bc options           open the options window
  /bc shout [channel]   post a one-line Bar Check to group chat (say, party, raid or guild)
  /bc reset             put the minimap button back on the minimap edge (and show it)
  /bc export            raw data window (for troubleshooting)
  Key binding           Game Menu > Options > Key Bindings > AddOns > Bar Check
  Minimap button        left-click = your card, right-click = saved characters. Drag it to move it.

ON THE CARD
  Characters   pick a saved character (or your current one)
  Bar Check!   post a one-line summary to party, raid or guild chat (say if you are alone)
  Copy Text    the card as plain text, ready to copy with Ctrl+C
  Options      animation on/off, faster animation, card size, guild name on the card, minimap button, update notice, and which stats show (no limit: the stats strip adds rows as needed)

The card builds itself in about 2.5 seconds. Click the card to skip. Esc closes it.
To share it, use your own snipping tool (Windows: Win+Shift+S) and paste it into Discord. WoW addons cannot copy images to the clipboard.

PRIVACY
Bar Check saves your character snapshot on your own computer (in your SavedVariables folder). The only thing it sends is its own version number (for example "V:1.2.0") to your guild or group, as a hidden addon message, so users can be told when a newer version exists. You can switch this off in Options. The Bar Check! button only types a line into the chat channel you are in, and only when you click it.
